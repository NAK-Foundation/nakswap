Before you swap

Minimum$25 per swap, or 0.1 ICPHoudiniswap won't quote below $25. Private routes usually need more, often $40 or so; the quote tells you. Paying with ICP directly starts at 0.1 ICP.
Maximum50 ICP per swapAbove this, the router refunds the ICP to your principal instead of buying NAK.
NAKSwap fee1% of the ICPTaken from the ICP that reaches the router, only when the swap to NAK goes through. Refunds carry no NAKSwap fee. The NAK amount you’re quoted already has it taken out, and Houdiniswap’s own fee and network costs are already in its quote.
Price guard3.5% price impactQuotes that would move the NAK price more than this are refused, with a smaller amount suggested. If the price moves past 5% by the time your ICP arrives, you get ICP back instead.
Timing1–45 minutesPaying with ICP: about a minute after your ICP arrives. Houdiniswap standard routes take about 5–30 minutes and private routes 15–45.
RefundsBack to youIf Houdiniswap can’t finish its step, Houdiniswap sends your token back to your refund address. If the ICP → NAK step can’t run, the router refunds the ICP to your principal.
PrivacyNo accountsNAKSwap has no sign-ups and keeps no profiles. To quote and route a Houdiniswap swap, your IP address, browser type and time zone are passed to Houdiniswap. NAK payouts are public on the ICP ledger, like any transfer.

Routing
Use the principal of a wallet you control, copied from its Receive screen, never from past transactions: scammers send tiny amounts from look-alike addresses hoping you’ll paste theirs.
